## What this Bible is

This is the single source of truth for the FLIP Burger digital restaurant experience. Every future design decision, every campaign, every photograph, and every line of code answers to this document set. It is written for a cloud brand whose only restaurant **is** the screen: there is no dining room, no signage, no counter — the website is the front door, the table, and the kitchen pass all at once.

The Bible is strategic and creative. It contains **no code**. Its technical companion, [EXPERIENCE-DESIGN-V2.md](../EXPERIENCE-DESIGN-V2.md), translates these decisions into implementation-level specification (tokens, files, budgets, acceptance criteria). A compressed one-file summary of this Bible exists as [EXPERIENCE-DESIGN-BIBLE-V3.md](../EXPERIENCE-DESIGN-BIBLE-V3.md) — the Executive Summary for fast onboarding.

## Precedence & governance

1. **This Bible wins.** Where any other document (including the V2 technical spec) disagrees with the Bible, the Bible's decision stands and the other document is revised to match.
2. **The Executive Summary follows the Bible.** It is a digest; it never introduces decisions of its own.
3. **Changes are deliberate.** A change to any chapter requires a reason recorded in that chapter's revision note — the Bible is stable by design, because a source of truth that drifts casually is not a source of truth.
4. **Copy approval discipline.** Every Arabic/English customer-facing string proposed anywhere in this Bible is **pending Mohamed's approval** until moved into the live content files. This mirrors the placeholder discipline already established in the project's content data.

## Honesty policy

This Bible never invents reality. Categories, channels, and capabilities that do not exist today — Chicken, Loaded Fries, Desserts, Kids, Table QR, NFC, sound design, AI content generation — are explicitly marked **[FUTURE]** wherever they appear. They are planned so the team never designs them cold under launch pressure, but they are never described as if they were live. The same honesty binds outward: no fake urgency, no fabricated social proof, no AI-generated food imagery — these are brand-protection laws, not style preferences.

## How to read this Bible

- **New team member:** read the Executive Summary first, then Chapters 1–3 (soul), then whichever chapter your discipline touches.
- **Designer starting a screen:** Chapter 3 (principles) → Chapter 4 (visual language) → the chapter for your screen (8 for hero/navigation/product, 9 for conversion/QR).
- **Photographer or videographer:** Chapter 6 end-to-end, plus Chapter 5 (color) and Chapter 7 (category personalities).
- **Marketer:** Chapters 1, 2, 7, 9.
- **Engineer:** this index, Chapter 3, Chapters 10–11 — then live in the V2 technical spec.

## The one-sentence Bible

If every page of this document were lost except one line, it should be this:

> **FLIP does not sell burgers online — it recreates the feeling of standing next to real fire, then gets out of the way so the customer can order.**
> **فليب ما يبيع برجر أونلاين — فليب يخليك تحس إنك واقف جنب نار حقيقية، وبعدها يفتح لك الطريق تطلب.**
